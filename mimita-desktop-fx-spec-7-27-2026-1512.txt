One idea I think would be especially unique: Effect Graph
Instead of a fixed pipeline, make every effect a node.
Desktop Capture
      |
Hue Shift
      |
Bloom
      |
Screen Wave
      |
Motion Trails
      |
Kaleidoscope
      |
CRT
      |
Display
Then allow users to reconnect them however they want:
Desktop
   |
RGB Split
   |
+--------------------+
|                    |
Bloom            Edge Detect
|                    |
+--------Multiply----+
         |
Temporal Trails
         |
Display
That turns it into a visual playground rather than just a settings menu. People could create and share entire effect graphs, much like shader packs or image-editing workflows. It also makes adding new effects much easier over time: each new effect is just another node users can wire into the pipeline.
